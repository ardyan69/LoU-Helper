Privacy Policy — LoU Revival Helper

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · applies to version 1.18.1 and later

Developer: ardhie — ardhie.andro@gmail.com

LoU Revival Helper ("the extension") is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari that helps players of the browser game Lord of Ultima: Revival (lordofultima.me). Its single purpose is to show and plan your own game data: city overview, layout planner, troop movements, dungeons, operations and minister settings.

In short: everything the extension reads is stored only in your own browser. The developer has no server, receives none of your data, and does not sell, share, track or advertise. The only thing the extension ever sends anywhere is minister settings you choose to apply, sent to the game itself.

1. What data the extension handles

a) Game data read from lordofultima.me

The extension only runs on pages of https://lordofultima.me/. There it reads data that the game already shows you or already holds in the page:

  • Your cities: names, coordinates, resources, storage capacity, production, buildings and layouts, building levels and build queues.
  • Your army and account progress: troops, recruitment queues, army movements (raids, attacks, supports, returns), trade convoys, research levels and minister settings.
  • Map data: dungeons and bosses (type, level, explored %), and other players' cities seen on the map (city name, coordinates, the player's in-game name and alliance tag, score).
  • Incoming attacks: time, target, attacker's in-game name and visible troops, kept as an attack history.
  • Game interface data: game icons and building names (to draw the layout planner), the game's language and the server time offset.

The extension does not read or store: your password or login details, cookies, session tokens, email address, payment details, in-game chat or messages, or any page outside lordofultima.me. It does not record your browsing history.

b) Data you enter yourself

Layout plans, notes and relation tags for players, raid notes, attack/defense plans, minister drafts, settings (language, notifications, unit values) and the inputs of the dashboard tools.

c) Derived data

Statistics calculated from the above, such as hourly snapshots of score, troops and production (up to 60 days), raid loot logs and speed calibration.

2. How the data is used

Only to provide the features you see in the extension: overviews, calculations, recommendations, countdowns and desktop notifications (for example a full warehouse, an incoming attack, or finished training). It is never used for advertising, profiling, credit or lending decisions, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

3. Where the data is stored

  • In your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local) and, for a few dashboard tool inputs, in the extension page's local storage.
  • Data does not sync to other devices and is not uploaded to any server owned by the developer or anyone else.
  • There are no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, cookies, advertising or third-party scripts.

4. What data is sent or shared

  • To the developer: nothing.
  • To third parties: nothing. Your data is not sold, rented or transferred.
  • To the game (lordofultima.me): only when you click 🎩 Set Ministers → Apply to game and confirm. The extension then sends the minister settings shown in the dialog (Build, Defense, War and Trade minister settings, and switching a minister on or off) over the game's own connection, using the same commands as the game's Ministers screen. Nothing else is ever sent to the game.
  • Backups: Settings → Export backup saves a JSON file to your own device when you click it. Where that file goes afterwards is up to you.
  • External links: some reference tables link to community websites (for example cotgopt.com). They open only when you click them, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Data stays in your browser until you delete it. You can:

  • delete everything at once with Settings → Delete all data;
  • clear the raid statistics or individual plans from their own screens;
  • remove the extension: the browser then deletes all of its stored data.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing stored elsewhere to request, correct or delete.

7. Security

Data never leaves your device except for the minister settings described above, which travel over the game's own secure (HTTPS/WSS) connection. The extension contains no remotely loaded code: everything it runs is included in the installed package.

8. Children

The extension is a tool for players of Lord of Ultima: Revival. It does not knowingly collect any personal information, from children or anyone else.

9. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from the game page by LoU Revival Helper adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose and its user-facing features;
  • data is not transferred to anyone, except the minister settings you explicitly apply to the game;
  • data is not used or transferred for personalized advertising;
  • no human reads your data, since the developer never receives it.

10. Changes to this policy

If the extension starts handling data differently, this policy is updated before that version is released, and the change is listed in the Change Log (ℹ️ About → 📝 Change Log) with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Kebijakan Privasi — LoU Revival Helper

Berlaku sejak: 30 Oktober 2026 · untuk versi 1.18.1 dan seterusnya

Pengembang: ardhie — ardhie.andro@gmail.com

LoU Revival Helper ("extension") adalah extension browser untuk Chrome, Edge, Firefox dan Safari yang membantu pemain game browser Lord of Ultima: Revival (lordofultima.me). Tujuan tunggalnya adalah menampilkan dan merencanakan data game milikmu sendiri: ringkasan kota, perencana layout, pergerakan pasukan, dungeon, operasi dan setelan minister.

Singkatnya: semua yang dibaca extension disimpan hanya di browser kamu sendiri. Pengembang tidak punya server, tidak menerima datamu, dan tidak menjual, membagikan, melacak, atau menampilkan iklan. Satu-satunya yang pernah dikirim extension adalah setelan minister yang kamu pilih untuk diterapkan, dan itu dikirim ke game itu sendiri.

1. Data yang ditangani extension

a) Data game yang dibaca dari lordofultima.me

Extension hanya berjalan di halaman https://lordofultima.me/. Di sana extension membaca data yang memang sudah ditampilkan atau dimuat oleh game:

  • Kota milikmu: nama, koordinat, resource, kapasitas gudang, produksi, bangunan dan layout, level bangunan, serta antrian bangun.
  • Pasukan dan kemajuan akun: pasukan, antrian rekrut, pergerakan pasukan (raid, serangan, bantuan, pulang), konvoi dagang, level riset dan setelan minister.
  • Data peta: dungeon dan boss (jenis, level, % explored), serta kota pemain lain yang terlihat di peta (nama kota, koordinat, nama pemain di game dan tag aliansi, skor).
  • Serangan masuk: waktu, target, nama penyerang di game dan pasukan yang terlihat, disimpan sebagai riwayat serangan.
  • Data tampilan game: ikon dan nama bangunan dari game (untuk menggambar perencana layout), bahasa game dan selisih jam server.

Extension tidak membaca atau menyimpan: password atau data login, cookie, token sesi, alamat email, data pembayaran, chat atau pesan di game, maupun halaman apa pun di luar lordofultima.me. Extension tidak merekam riwayat browsing kamu.

b) Data yang kamu isi sendiri

Rencana layout, catatan dan tag relasi untuk pemain, catatan raid, rencana serangan/bertahan, draf minister, pengaturan (bahasa, notifikasi, nilai unit) dan isian alat-alat di dashboard.

c) Data turunan

Statistik yang dihitung dari data di atas, misalnya catatan per jam untuk skor, pasukan dan produksi (sampai 60 hari), log loot raid dan kalibrasi kecepatan.

2. Cara data digunakan

Hanya untuk menjalankan fitur yang kamu lihat di extension: ringkasan, perhitungan, rekomendasi, hitung mundur dan notifikasi desktop (misalnya gudang penuh, serangan masuk, atau pelatihan selesai). Data tidak pernah dipakai untuk iklan, profiling, keputusan kredit/pinjaman, atau tujuan lain di luar fitur tersebut.

3. Tempat data disimpan

  • Di penyimpanan extension browser kamu (chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local), dan untuk beberapa isian alat dashboard, di local storage halaman extension.
  • Data tidak disinkronkan ke perangkat lain dan tidak diunggah ke server milik pengembang maupun pihak lain.
  • Tidak ada analytics, telemetri, pelaporan crash, cookie, iklan, atau skrip pihak ketiga.

4. Data yang dikirim atau dibagikan

  • Ke pengembang: tidak ada.
  • Ke pihak ketiga: tidak ada. Datamu tidak dijual, disewakan, atau dipindahtangankan.
  • Ke game (lordofultima.me): hanya saat kamu klik 🎩 Set Ministers → Terapkan ke game dan mengonfirmasi. Extension lalu mengirim setelan minister yang tampil di dialog (setelan Build, Defense, War dan Trade minister, serta menyalakan/mematikan minister) lewat koneksi game itu sendiri, memakai perintah yang sama dengan layar Ministers di game. Tidak ada hal lain yang dikirim ke game.
  • Cadangan: Pengaturan → Ekspor cadangan menyimpan file JSON ke perangkatmu sendiri saat kamu klik. Ke mana file itu dibawa selanjutnya, kamu yang menentukan.
  • Tautan luar: beberapa tabel referensi menautkan ke situs komunitas (misalnya cotgopt.com). Situs itu hanya terbuka saat kamu klik, dan punya kebijakan privasinya sendiri.

6. Penyimpanan dan penghapusan data

Data tetap ada di browser kamu sampai kamu menghapusnya. Kamu bisa:

  • menghapus semuanya sekaligus lewat Pengaturan → Hapus semua data;
  • menghapus statistik raid atau rencana tertentu dari layarnya masing-masing;
  • mencopot extension: browser lalu menghapus semua data yang disimpannya.

Karena pengembang tidak pernah menerima datamu, tidak ada data yang tersimpan di tempat lain untuk diminta, dikoreksi, atau dihapus.

7. Keamanan

Data tidak pernah keluar dari perangkatmu, kecuali setelan minister di atas, yang dikirim lewat koneksi aman (HTTPS/WSS) milik game. Extension tidak memuat kode dari luar: semua yang dijalankan sudah ada di paket yang terpasang.

8. Anak-anak

Extension ini adalah alat untuk pemain Lord of Ultima: Revival. Extension tidak dengan sengaja mengumpulkan informasi pribadi, baik dari anak-anak maupun siapa pun.

9. Limited Use Chrome Web Store

Penggunaan informasi yang diterima dari halaman game oleh LoU Revival Helper mematuhi Kebijakan Data Pengguna Chrome Web Store, termasuk persyaratan Limited Use:

  • data hanya dipakai untuk tujuan tunggal extension dan fitur-fitur yang terlihat oleh pengguna;
  • data tidak dipindahkan ke siapa pun, kecuali setelan minister yang kamu terapkan sendiri ke game;
  • data tidak dipakai atau dipindahkan untuk iklan yang dipersonalisasi;
  • tidak ada manusia yang membaca datamu, karena pengembang tidak pernah menerimanya.

10. Perubahan kebijakan
